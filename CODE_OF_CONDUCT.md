# Code of conduct

Snare is a community project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull
requests, reviews, and chat) is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good faith;
- critique code and ideas, not people;
- keep discussion on topic and free of harassment, insults, and personal or
  sexual attention;
- respect privacy: never post someone else's personal information, and never
  post real user data or live credentials, even in a bug report;
- not use the project's spaces to plan or encourage attacks on systems the
  participant is not authorized to test.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the
repository profile, or through a GitHub Security Advisory if the report also
involves a security issue. Maintainers will review every report, keep the
reporter's identity confidential, and may warn, temporarily ban, or
permanently ban participants who break these rules.

This text is intentionally short. Maintainers may adopt the Contributor
Covenant in its place.
